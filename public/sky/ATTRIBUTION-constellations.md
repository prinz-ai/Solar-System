# Western Constellation Figure Attribution

The constellation figures in `constellation-figures.json` are adapted from
the Western (Sky & Telescope) sky culture in Stellarium Sky Cultures:

https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures/tree/master/western_SnT

Original line figures: Paul Krizak and Jonathan E. Piskor.
Subsequent rework: the Stellarium team.

The figure data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0:

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/

The generated catalog adds resolved Hipparcos star coordinates, magnitudes,
and B-V colors from D3-Celestial's `stars.14.json`:

https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial

D3-Celestial is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license in
`LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt`.
